Field Sheet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains what information Field Sheet collects, how it's used, and what choices you have. It applies alongside our Terms of Service.

1. What We Collect

Account information: the email and password you sign up with, and your chosen username. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider (Supabase) and never stored in plain text.

Contribution data: any spec, value, or source citation you submit is stored and displayed publicly along with your username and timestamp — this is by design, since Field Sheet's trust model depends on visible, attributable sourcing.

Automatically collected information: our hosting providers may log standard technical data (IP address, browser type, request timestamps) for security purposes. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

Session data: your browser stores a session token locally to keep you signed in.

2. What We Don't Collect

We don't collect payment information, government ID, or firearms-ownership records tied to individuals. Field Sheet is a specifications reference, not a transaction or ownership-tracking platform.

3. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with Supabase (our database/auth provider) and Vercel (our hosting provider), both acting as infrastructure on our behalf, or if required by valid legal process.

4. Your Choices and Rights

Access: view your data by browsing your own contribution history. Correction: update your username anytime, or correct a fact by adding a new sourced entry (we don't allow silently editing past submissions, to preserve an honest history). Deletion: request account deletion at [CONTACT EMAIL] — your personal account info will be removed, though past contributed facts may remain attributed to a deleted account to preserve the integrity of shared records. Opt-out: we don't send marketing email; account-related email can't be opted out of while your account is active.

If you're located somewhere with specific statutory privacy rights (EU/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA), contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we'll do our best to accommodate applicable requests.

5. Children's Privacy

Field Sheet is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we will terminate it and delete associated personal information.

6. Security

We rely on database-level Row Level Security to restrict data access, and route all writes through validated functions rather than open access. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security of submitted information.

7. Data Retention

We retain account information while your account is active. Public contribution data may be retained indefinitely as part of the Service's historical record, consistent with Section 4 above.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date above.

9. Contact

Questions or requests regarding your personal information can be sent to [CONTACT EMAIL].
