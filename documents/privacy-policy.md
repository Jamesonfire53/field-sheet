# Field Sheet — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

This Privacy Policy explains what information Field Sheet collects, how it's used, and what choices you have. It applies to the Field Sheet website and is meant to be read alongside our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md).

## 1. What We Collect

**Account information.** When you sign up, we collect the email address and password you provide, and the username you choose. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they're handled by our authentication provider (Supabase), which stores them using industry-standard hashing.

**Contribution data.** Any spec, value, or source citation you submit is stored and displayed publicly as part of the Service, along with your username and the time of submission. This is by design — Field Sheet's trust model depends on visible, attributable sourcing. Do not submit personal information about yourself or others as part of a source citation.

**Automatically collected information.** Our hosting and infrastructure providers (see Section 3) may automatically log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps, for security and abuse-prevention purposes. We do not currently use analytics or advertising trackers.

**Session data.** To keep you signed in, your browser stores a session token locally (via your browser's storage, not a third-party tracking cookie). This is used only to authenticate your requests to the Service.

## 2. What We Don't Collect

We do not collect payment information (Field Sheet doesn't process purchases), government-issued ID, or any firearms-ownership records tied to specific individuals. Field Sheet is a specifications reference, not a transaction or ownership-tracking platform.

## 3. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

- **Supabase** (our database and authentication provider), which stores your account and submission data on our behalf.
- **Vercel** (our hosting provider), which serves the website and may log basic request data for performance and security.
- **Law enforcement or legal process**, only if required by valid legal request, and limited to what's legally required.

Both Supabase and Vercel are bound by their own privacy and security practices as our infrastructure providers; we encourage you to review their policies if you'd like more detail on their handling of hosted data.

## 4. Your Choices and Rights

- **Access/export**: You can view all data associated with your account by browsing your own contribution history on the Service.
- **Correction**: You can update your username through your account, and correct a submitted fact by adding a new sourced entry (Field Sheet doesn't allow silently editing past submissions, to preserve an honest history — see Section 4 of the Terms of Service).
- **Deletion**: You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information (email, login credentials) by contacting **[CONTACT EMAIL]**. Note that your past contributions (submitted facts and source citations) may remain on the Service in de-identified or attributed-to-deleted-user form, since removing verified factual data would compromise the integrity of shared records other users rely on — but we will remove your email and any other personally identifying account information upon request.
- **Opt-out**: We don't send marketing email. Account-related email (confirmation, password reset) is necessary to operate the Service and can't be opted out of while keeping an active account.

If you're located somewhere with specific statutory privacy rights (e.g. the EU/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA), contact us at **[CONTACT EMAIL]** and we'll do our best to accommodate applicable requests; note this policy has not yet been reviewed for full regulatory compliance in every jurisdiction — see the note at the end of this document.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Field Sheet is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under 18 (see Section 2 of the Terms of Service). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we will terminate it and delete associated personal information.

## 6. Security

We rely on Supabase's Row Level Security to restrict what data is readable or writable, and route all data writes through validated functions rather than open database access. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security of information you submit.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. Public contribution data (facts and sources, not tied to personally identifying information beyond your username) may be retained indefinitely as part of the Service's historical record, consistent with Section 4 above.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your personal information, can be sent to **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.
